# Governance

CloudNativePatroni is a personal project maintained by one individual. It has no
community governance structure, and it would be dishonest to publish one at
this stage.

Decisions are made by the individual maintainer.
Architectural decisions are recorded as architecture decision records under
`docs/adr/` and are binding once accepted. Where an owner directive and the
architecture specification disagree, the directive wins and the deviation is
recorded in the affected document.

A governance model will be published if and when the project has contributors
beyond its individual maintainer, and it will be written for this project rather
than inherited. Until then, see [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md) for the
maintenance model and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to
contribute.

> CloudNativePatroni is an independent project derived from CloudNativePG. It is not affiliated
> with or endorsed by CloudNativePG, CNCF, LF Projects, or the Patroni maintainers.
